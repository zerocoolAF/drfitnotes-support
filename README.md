# DriftNotes Support

Support repository for DriftNotes - the note-taking app where notes disappear when you don't need them anymore.

## Get Help


### Report a Bug
[Create a new issue](../../issues/new) with:
- Your device model and iOS version
- Steps to reproduce the problem
- What you expected to happen vs what actually happened
- Screenshots if helpful

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I make notes expire?
When creating or editing a note, tap the clock icon to set how long the note should last (30 minutes to 2 weeks, or forever).

### How do I pin important notes?
Tap the pin icon on any note to prevent it from expiring. Pinned notes stay until you manually unpin them.

### How do I set up location reminders?
1. Tap the location icon when creating a note
2. Choose a location or add a new one
3. The note will appear when you arrive at that location

### How do I use Siri with DriftNotes?
Say "Hey Siri, add a drift note" followed by your note content.

### My notes aren't syncing between devices
DriftNotes stores all data locally on your device for privacy. Notes don't sync between devices.

## Privacy
DriftNotes keeps all your data on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit your notes anywhere.
