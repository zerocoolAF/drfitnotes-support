# Privacy Policy for DriftNotes

Last updated: September 27, 2025

## Overview

DriftNotes is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.** DriftNotes is designed with privacy in mind:

- All your notes are stored locally on your device
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- We don't use analytics or tracking tools
- We don't collect usage statistics
- We don't access your personal information

## Data Storage

- All notes are stored securely on your device using SwiftData
- Notes are automatically deleted based on their expiration settings
- Your data never leaves your device

## Location Services

- Location data is only used locally on your device for location-based reminders
- Location information is never transmitted to external servers
- You can disable location services at any time in iOS Settings

## Notifications

- If you enable notifications, they are sent locally by your device
- No notification data is transmitted to external services
- You can disable notifications at any time in Settings

## Children's Privacy

DriftNotes does not collect any information from users of any age. The app is safe for children to use.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
- Email: privacy@pixeldrift.studio
